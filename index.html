About the Practice

Two kinds of knowledge

Analytical skill tells you what the data shows. Subject-matter understanding tells you what it means.

Glorious Morning Consulting is built on both. The practice combines rigorous analytical and programming methods with genuine engagement in the subjects being analyzed — environmental policy, public finance, place, community, housing, and the policy questions that cut across all of them.

The result is work that doesn't just produce accurate findings, but explains why they matter and what they imply for the question at hand.
